Module Overview

The Machine Learning module explores the fundamental theories, mathematical concepts and algorithms underpinning data-driven intelligent systems, focusing on their practical implementation and application to real-world analytical problems. It examines various learning paradigms, predictive models and optimisation techniques using appropriate software tools, alongside methods for evaluating model performance, interpretability and reliability. Through practical programming activities, collaborative discussions and reflective exercises, the module also develops essential technical and professional skills, including critical thinking, problem-solving, data analysis and ethical awareness, with an emphasis on systematic model development, responsible AI practices and effective deployment in real-world environments.

Learning Outcomes

Throughout this module, I developed the knowledge and skills required to:

  • Articulate the legal, social, ethical and professional issues faced by machine learning professionals.
  • Understand the applicability and challenges associated with different datasets for the use of machine learning algorithms.
  • Apply and critically appraise machine learning techniques to real-world problems, particularly where technical risk and uncertainty is involved.
  • Systematically develop and implement the skills required to be effective member of a development team in a virtual professional environment, adopting real-life perspectives on team roles and organisation.

E-Portfolio Activities

01

Collaborative Discussions

Explore my contributions to collaborative discussions, demonstrating critical thinking, knowledge sharing and meaningful engagement with peers through the analysis, evaluation and exchange of ideas on key module topics.

 02

Correlation and Regression

Explore correlation and regression techniques to analyse relationships between variables, identify data patterns, and develop predictive models, demonstrating their importance in statistical analysis and machine learning.

 03

Jaccard Distance / Dissimilarity Calculations

Explore Jaccard distance and dissimilarity calculations to measure differences between datasets, evaluate similarities, and identify patterns, demonstrating their importance in data comparison, clustering, and machine learning.

 04

Perceptron Activities

Explore the implementation of simple and multi-layer perceptrons using Python, examining logical operations, activation functions, and neural network structures to understand their applications and challenges in machine learning.

 05

Gradient Cost Function

Explore the application of gradient-based optimisation and cost functions to measure prediction errors, adjust model parameters, and improve accuracy, demonstrating their importance in model training and machine learning.

 06

CNN Model Activity

Explore the implementation of Convolutional Neural Networks for image recognition, evaluating prediction accuracy, dataset challenges, and the ethical and social implications of CNN technology in machine learning.

 07

Model Performance

Explore the evaluation of machine learning model performance by adjusting parameters and analysing their impact on AUC and R² metrics, demonstrating their importance in assessing predictive accuracy and model reliability.

 08

Wiki Entries

Explore my contributions to the Module Wiki, sharing key machine learning concepts, definitions, and research findings to support collaborative knowledge development and deepen understanding of emerging topics.

Evaluation & Reflection
