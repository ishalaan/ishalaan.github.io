Module Overview

The Intelligent Agents module explores the models, architectures and techniques underpinning agent-based systems, focusing on their critical evaluation and application to real-world problems involving risk and uncertainty. It examines the design and implementation of intelligent agents using appropriate software tools, alongside the technical, legal, social, ethical and professional considerations associated with their deployment. Through collaborative discussions, practical team exercises and reflective activities, the module also develops essential professional skills, including critical thinking, problem-solving, communication, teamwork, leadership and ethical awareness, with an emphasis on effective collaboration in virtual development environments.

Learning Outcomes

Throughout this module, I developed the knowledge and skills required to:

  • Identify and critically analyse agent-based systems, differentiating between architectures and approaches.
  • Apply and critically evaluate intelligent agent techniques to real-world problems, particularly where technical risk and uncertainty is involved.
  • Deploy critically appropriate software tools and skills for the design and implementation of an agent-based system, bearing in mind applicable legal, social, ethical, and professional issues.
  • Systematically develop and implement the skills required to be effective member of a development team in a virtual professional environment, adopting real-life perspectives on team roles and organisation.

E-Portfolio Activities

 02

Creating Agent Dialogues

Explore the creation of agent dialogues using KQML and KIF, demonstrating communication, information exchange and coordination between intelligent agents in a warehouse stock management scenario.

 03

Creating Parse Trees

Explore the creation of constituency-based parse trees, demonstrating syntactic analysis, sentence structure interpretation and the application of natural language processing techniques to support intelligent agent systems.

 04

Deep Learning in Action

Explore the ethical and safety challenges surrounding intelligent agents, examining responsible AI deployment, risk mitigation and critical evaluation through academic research, reflective discussion and peer collaboration.
