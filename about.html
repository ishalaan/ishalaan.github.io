Introduction

I am a Group Technical Director with 18 years of software development experience and a career spanning data analysis, database management and technical leadership. My strengths lie in solution architecture, analytical problem-solving and translating complex business requirements into practical software and data solutions.

My technical background includes PHP, JavaScript, Python, CSS, MySQL and SQL, supported by four to five years of experience implementing AI and machine learning in a professional setting. I combine hands-on development with a broader architectural perspective, considering maintainability, reliability and business value alongside technical performance.

I am currently studying for an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at the University of Essex, building on a Postgraduate Certificate awarded with Distinction. This complements my industry experience with deeper expertise in intelligent systems, rigorous evaluation and responsible AI development.
