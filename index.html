Introduction

This e-portfolio brings together selected activities, projects and reflections from my postgraduate studies in Artificial Intelligence. It traces how my understanding develops as I connect academic theory with practical implementation and critically examine the decisions behind my work.

With an established background in software development and technical leadership, I bring practical experience to my studies while using the programme to question familiar approaches and strengthen the reasoning behind them. My focus extends beyond building systems that work to understanding how they should be evaluated, where their limitations lie and what their use means for individuals and organisations.

Each module section includes coursework, practical outputs, collaborative contributions and reflections on challenges, feedback and lessons learned. Together, these provide a record of my academic progress and show how this learning informs my professional judgement and future development.
